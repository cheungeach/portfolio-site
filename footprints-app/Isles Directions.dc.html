Footprints

A map where every place is a small floating isle carrying the traces of what people did there. Lists (“100 things before I graduate”) pin things to isles; Lumen, a wordless light, guides you through what others left behind, recognises your photos when you check in, and turns the day into a postcard.

Assumptions
UC Berkeley, English copy. You are signed in as Mia, author of the 100-things list. The map is real geography (drag to pan) with a hand-drawn ink treatment over it — street and place labels stay readable. Landmarks stand on the map as inked isometric buildings at true coordinates (tower, colonnaded library, awninged shopfronts, a mound for the Big C), count flag above, name below; they scale with zoom and labels yield when crowded. Apple/Google 3D tiles need a billing key, so Esri street tiles stand in and building shapes are per-place types rather than derived from real 3D footprints. Photo recognition is simulated with per-place tags.
Design system
Broadsheet: Source Serif 4 as the chrome, cyan for interaction, magenta as the rare stamp color, 2px radii, whitespace instead of dividers. The hand-drawn softness lives only in the isles, the sprite and the postcard.
Clickable flow
Sign in → map → tap Sather Tower → a person → their lists → check in (take photo → Lumen recognises → leave a trace) → “Make postcard”. Tabs, guide chips, list checkboxes and “New list” all work.
Three directions
Same prototype, three skins — 1a newsprint paper, 1b dawn sky (closest to Sky’s palette), 1c ink-and-wash outlines. Pick one and I’ll deepen it.
